**Notes**
- S02 labels the 11 kW wallbox as single-phase, which conflicts with S01 (pp. 7–8: 11 kW is three-phase). S01 prevails.
- S01 (p. 14) references annex ed1.2; this project uses the newer ed1.4 (S02).
- S09: "Global" excludes China (p. 3); the dataset ends at wire rod (about 8 mm), so wire drawing and annealing are not included (p. 6). Critically reviewed per ISO 14044 and ISO/TS 14071 (p. 2).
- S10–S14: selected from S15 as the wall-mounted AC wallboxes with an attached cable (20 AC private or semi-public PEPs screened; only S10 is single-phase). Harmonized composition: `data/processed/pep_wallbox_composition.csv`.
- S12: the "Electrical wire (high current)" share is 27.4%, derived from the published category totals.
- S13: cable mass is not reported separately; PVC plus all copper is assigned to the cable as an upper-bound estimate. The PEP appendix shows socket variants at 0.70–0.77 of the tethered model's manufacturing impact.
- S14: the use stage assumes a 2 Ω contact resistance, giving 7,870 kWh of losses (28% of energy delivered), which is not physically plausible; its manufacturing result (220 kg CO₂e) is an outlier and likely reflects a different database (ecoinvent). Only its material composition is used.
- S16: public specifications are used only as one reference point for product mass; this project does not represent Delta's products or data. The net weight "depends on model configuration" (48 A or 80 A) and the datasheet does not state whether it includes the cable. To verify on Delta's own website.
- S17: a non-functional demo sample; a working connector may weigh more.
- S18: the report warns that it represents only the participating companies (about 21% of EU PA6 capacity).
- PlasticsEurope PC eco-profile (March 2019): listed on https://plasticseurope.org/sustainability/circularity/life-cycle-thinking/eco-profiles-set/ but the file on legacy.plasticseurope.org could not be downloaded on 2026-10-22; see A10.
- S04–S07: cable details were read from each original document. Products with only retailer listings (no manufacturer or certification source) were not included.
